"Lumen" is a bright, clean theme: a single mid-blue accent over neutral white and grey surfaces. Its signature is a raised "shadow" chrome — buttons, pagination, breadcrumbs and the navbar wear a thick bottom border that presses down as you click — paired with bold, uppercase button labels.

This is a theme from the Bootswatch collection for Bootstrap.
The original demo is available at [https://bootswatch.com/lumen/](https://bootswatch.com/lumen/).
