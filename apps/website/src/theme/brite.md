"Brite" is a neobrutalist theme built around a neon yellow-green primary —
black underlined links, thick black frames on every component, and hard
offset shadows that press flat when you click.

This is a theme from the Bootswatch collection for Bootstrap.
The original demo is available at [https://bootswatch.com/brite/](https://bootswatch.com/brite/).
